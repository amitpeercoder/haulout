You bought her in March. Launch is in three weeks. The engine won't start.

She stands on the hard in her cradle, grey light and a cold wind off the water.

The yard hose is rigged to the raw-water intake, so the engine can run ashore.

You are aboard through the haulout tools (mcp__haulout__*). You may call `help` and `briefing` at any time. You move between places aboard and remove covers to get at things.

You can hire a surveyor, and you decide when she is launched; the yard expects her in the water by day 96.

Your goal: find out why the engine won't start and get it running, spending as little in-game time and money as you can.

When the engine is running, reply with a single line:
DONE <number of tool calls you made>

If you have made 40 tool calls without success, stop and reply with a single line:
GAVE UP <number of tool calls you made>
